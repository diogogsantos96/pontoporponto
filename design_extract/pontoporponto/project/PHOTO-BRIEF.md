## Tone & colour guidance (all images)

- **Soft, natural light.** Avoid hard flash or studio strobes if possible.
- **Warm, neutral palette.** Cream, ivory, soft taupe, blush, sage, dusty blue. Stay away from saturated reds/greens/yellows.
- **Shallow depth of field** on detail shots — fabric, hands, buttons — to feel intimate.
- **Empty space matters** — the layout uses image edges for text overlays. Don't fill every corner of every shot.
- **Consistency.** Same photographer, same week of shooting, same light setup as much as possible. It's worth more than a great variety of styles.

JPEG quality 80–85, sRGB colour space, file sizes ideally under 400 KB each for fast page loads.
